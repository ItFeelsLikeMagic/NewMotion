NewMotion Support

Help for the iPhone app and the Mac companion

Get help

Ask a question, report a bug, or request a feature by opening an issue at github.com/davlia/NewMotion/issues. Every issue is read and answered there.

Getting started

  1. Install the Mac companion. Paste this into Terminal:
    curl -fsSL https://davlia.github.io/NewMotion/install.sh | sh
    Or download NewMotion.dmg from Releases and drag it into Applications.
  2. Open NewMotion on the Mac and follow its setup screens.
  3. Open the iPhone app and scan the QR code the Mac shows. After that the phone reconnects on its own.

Common problems

The phone will not connect. Make sure Bluetooth is on for both devices and NewMotion is running in the Mac menu bar. Keep the phone within a few meters of the Mac.

The pointer does not move or nothing types. Open System Settings on the Mac, go to Privacy and Security, then Accessibility, and turn NewMotion on.

Typing stops in some fields. NewMotion never types into a password field. This is on purpose.

Dictation does nothing. Allow microphone and speech access for NewMotion in the iPhone Settings app.

Uninstall the Mac companion

  1. Quit NewMotion from the menu bar.
  2. Drag NewMotion from Applications to the Trash.
  3. Remove it in System Settings under Privacy and Security, in both Accessibility and Bluetooth.
