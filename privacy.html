NewMotion Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

NewMotion is an iPhone app and a Mac companion that let the phone drive the Mac over Bluetooth LE. This policy covers both.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no servers of ours. We never see how you use either app.

Where your data goes

Everything travels over Bluetooth LE between your own iPhone and your own Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

The only network request either app makes is the Mac companion asking GitHub once a day whether a newer release exists, using the Sparkle updater. That request carries no personal data and no information about how you use the app.

Speech and dictation

Speech is transcribed on the iPhone by Apple's on-device speech engine. Audio never leaves the phone, and it is not recorded or saved.

What is never stored or logged

Typed text, transcripts, pointer movements, and audio are never stored and never written to a log. Debug output carries counts and states only.

Password fields

The Mac refuses to type anything while macOS reports a secure input session, so nothing is injected into a password field.

Permissions the iPhone asks for

  • Microphone: only while you hold Push to Talk, for dictation.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to your Mac.
  • Camera: only to scan the one-time pairing QR code shown by the Mac. No images are saved.
  • Motion: only while you hold Air Mouse, to move the pointer.

Pairing keys

The keys that let your two devices trust each other stay in the device Keychain. They never leave the device they were made on.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected.

Children

NewMotion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes to this policy

Any changes are published on this page, with a new effective date.
